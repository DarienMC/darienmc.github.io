## Game Jam Details
The GMTK Game Jam is an online annual event where game developers of all skill levels from all over join together to create video games within a strict time limit. 

I participated in the [GMTK Game Jam 2024](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2024) solo to see if I could use the Godot Engine to finish a project from A to Z efficiently. This year's theme was **Built to Scale**.

Once the theme was revealed, I decided to make a simple auto-runner where a robot scales a mountain while jumping over boulders of random sizes.

## Play in your Browser
If you are on your desktop, you can play the game right now by going to my Itch.io page [here](https://darienmc.itch.io/bouldering-bounce). 

A brief breakdown of the project is on that page as well, so I encourage you to read it.

## My Submission
If you want to see what other participants had to say about my submission, you can view their ratings and comments [here](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2024/rate/2904388)!
